# Board is OLED black or a white card

The Board hangs over the desktop, not on a page, so `--surface-raised` (grey chip) is the wrong elevation. Dark is OLED `#000` (instrument). Light is a white card on paper. Hairline and 8px radius stay. We considered the skill's dropdown recipe (`#1A1A1A` / `#F0F0F0`) and rejected it — Nothing's own dark is true black, and a grey plate on a light Mac desktop reads as disabled.
