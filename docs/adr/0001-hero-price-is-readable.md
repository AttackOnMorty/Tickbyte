# Hero price is readable, not dotted

The Board's Hero price is the first thing you read. Doto (the Motif) is a grid of holes — at 48px it loses to the 12px Glance. We keep Space Mono on every live number, including the Hero price, and leave the Motif on the app icon.

**Considered:** Doto on the Board because "the Glance already showed the price." Rejected: people open the Board to read that number better, not to see a poster of it.
