# Tickbyte

<img src="AppStore/Screenshots/Tickbyte-1440x900-v5.png" alt="Tickbyte: live BTC and ETH in the Mac menu bar" width="720" />

Live Bitcoin and Ethereum prices in the Mac menu bar. Trades are pushed from Binance as they happen — no polling, no account, no main window.

Click the status item for a larger view of one coin and today’s move. Click the other coin to bring it forward.

**[Get Tickbyte on the Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/search?term=Tickbyte%20Live%20Crypto%20Prices)**

Requires macOS 13 or later. Market data from Binance; informational only, not investment advice.

Privacy: Tickbyte does not collect personal data. Support: [support.dazeapp@gmail.com](mailto:support.dazeapp@gmail.com)
