# Tickbyte

A menu-bar instrument for live crypto prices. The bar is the glance; the dropdown is the board.

## Language

**Glance**:
The status-item title. Two small prices, always readable, no motif.
_Avoid_: ticker, menu, status text

**Board**:
The dropdown under the Glance. One focused coin is primary; the other is a compact row.
_Avoid_: detail, panel-as-poster, popup, menu

**Hero price**:
The one large number on the Board. It is what you read first. It is a price, not a brand moment.
_Avoid_: dotted number, display, motif, hero moment

**Motif**:
The dotted B on the app icon. Recognition, not data.
_Avoid_: Doto-on-price, NDot, Nothing number
