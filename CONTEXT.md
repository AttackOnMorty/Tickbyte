# Tickbyte

A menu-bar instrument for live crypto prices. The bar is the glance; the dropdown is the board.

## Language

**Glance**:
The status-item title. Two small prices, always readable, no motif.
_Avoid_: ticker, menu, status text

**Board**:
The dropdown under the Glance. One focused coin is primary; the other is a compact row. Dark is an instrument on black; light is a white card on paper.
_Avoid_: detail, panel-as-poster, popup, menu, raised grey chip

**Hero price**:
The one large number on the Board. It is what you read first. It is a price, not a brand moment. It is the only full-ink number on the Board. A new print does not change its colour.
_Avoid_: dotted number, display, motif, hero moment, two full-ink prices, last-print flash

**Motif**:
The dotted B on the app icon. Recognition, not data.
_Avoid_: Doto-on-price, NDot, Nothing number

**Tape**:
Print colour on a change. An advance is red; a decline is green. Flat is not a move.
_Avoid_: success-green-up, Western portfolio colours

**Wordmark**:
The brand name on the Board footer. Quiet Grotesk, not an instrument label.
_Avoid_: tracked mono legend, TICKBYTE-as-label, 13px footer type
